How Candy Crush is played, in plain words.

Candy Crush is a colorful match-three puzzle. This page gives you helpful information about the basic rules, special candies and level goals, so you can understand it quickly. Find useful information and explore available options below.

Learn More Online

The basic idea

A simple and convenient format: a board filled with candies, and a limited number of moves.

1

Swap two neighbors

Pick a candy and swap it with the one next to it, up, down, left or right.

2

Match 3 or more

Line up three or more candies of the same color in a row or column to clear them.

3

Watch the cascade

New candies drop in from above and can create extra matches on their own.

4

Reach the goal

Finish the level target before your moves run out to move ahead.

Common level goals

Explore your options: each level has its own target, shown before you begin.

🎯

Reach a score

Collect enough points within the allowed moves.

🍮

Clear the jelly

Match candies on top of every jelly square on the board.

🍒

Bring down ingredients

Move items like cherries to the bottom edge of the board.

🎨

Collect an order

Gather a set number of specific candies or colors.

Smart tips for beginners

Start exploring today with a few easy habits.

👀

Scan the whole board

Look for the biggest match before you make a move.

⬇️

Work from the bottom

Matches near the bottom shake up more candies and may trigger bonus chains.

💡

Save special candies

Combining two special candies often gives a stronger effect than using them alone.

⏸️

Read the goal first

Always check the level target so no move is wasted.
